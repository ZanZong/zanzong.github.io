Aslarge language models (LLMs) have become foundational components in diverse applications ranging from real-time chatbots to batch-oriented code generation, their deployment increasingly takes the form of LLM-as-a-Service (LLMaaS). This paradigm introduces service level objectives (SLOs) for LLM services, including Time to First To ken (TTFT), Time Per Output Token (TPOT), and end-to-end latency (e2e). The throughput of requests that satisfy their SLO constraints is measured as goodput. However, existing LLM inference services fail to adequately address the diverse and often conflicting SLOs of mixed workloads. Variable batch composition, prefill padding overhead, and fine-grained decode dynamics can jointly aggravate SLO violations and degrade the overall goodput of LLM services. In this paper, we propose GoodServe, an SLO-aware scheduling framework that improves good put for serving mixed LLM inference workloads. GoodServe coordinates a bucket padding optimizer, a slack time estimator, and a goodput-aware scheduler to jointly optimize prefill prompt grouping and decode request scheduling through a shared SLO slack. It reduces TTFT violations caused by excessive prefill padding and mitigates TPOT and e2e vio lations during decoding. Experiments on chatbot and code generation workloads with the default Llama/A800 setting show that GoodServe improves normalized goodput by 1.80× over vLLM, outperforms the SLO-aware baseline, and retains gains across additional models and GPUs.
