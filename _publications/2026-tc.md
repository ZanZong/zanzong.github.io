Multimodal encoders are widely used in multimodal tasks like visual question answering, text-to-video generation, etc. For the training of multimodal encoders, existing training systems primarily focus on individually adjusting parallel strategies to align the computation time of different modalities. However, due to the various parameter numbers of modalities, achieving computation balance through coarse-grained parallelism adjustment is nearly infeasible, which leads to low utilization of computing hardware.

To tackle this problem, we present ModalPlex, a parallel training system with modality awareness that enhances training efficiency. Inspired by the observations of unbalanced resource usage in pipeline parallelism, we explore the hybrid partitioning strategy of modalities, and further multiplex computing and memory resources within the pipeline execution. Instead of striving for the perfect computation balance of different modalities, our system provides sophisticated control of the modality’s partition and placement. Then, fine-grained scheduling makes use of the idle resources of the pipeline parallelism, which also completes the modality interaction with the pipeline scheduling. In evaluations, we compare the training efficiency between existing parallel training systems and ModalPlex. For 6 typical multimodal encoders, our system achieves up to 1.43× training throughput and good scalability when training on multiple GPU servers.
